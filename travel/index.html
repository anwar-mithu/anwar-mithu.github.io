Sample Journal Entry

A City of Layers

Berlin feels like several cities occupying the same space. Modern architecture, older stone, trains, street life and changing light create frames that are never quite the same twice. The most interesting photographs are often the quieter ones: a nearly empty platform, a reflection after rain, or evening light falling between buildings.

Sample Journal Entry

Along the Rhine

Cologne is dominated by its cathedral, but the Rhine changes the perspective. Bridges, trains, boats and pedestrians create constantly moving scenes. From across the river, the cathedral becomes part of a wider skyline — a strong place to explore composition, light and scale.

Sample Journal Entry

Quiet Outside the City

Forests reward patience. Paths, mist, leaves and changing light reveal themselves slowly, which makes photography feel different from working at a screen. Sometimes stepping away from technology is exactly what makes it easier to return to technical work with a clearer mind.
