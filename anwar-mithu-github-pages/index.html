01 / About
AI

I'm Md Anwarul Islam Mithu, an AI Engineer based in Germany with a background in Information Technology and a strong interest in building practical intelligent systems.

My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, software engineering and data systems. I am especially interested in applications where language models interact with tools, APIs, databases and software environments rather than functioning only as standalone conversational models.

My technical interests include language models, agentic AI, AI evaluation, explainable AI, local model deployment, analytical data systems and AI infrastructure.

My approach is practical: build it, test it, measure it, understand why it fails and improve it.
